Proper insurance coverage, coupled with a firm adherence to the relevant hurrican-aware building codes(descended from those laid down when Hibert Saffir of Saffir-Simpson-scale fame was a county engineer in south Florida) could help make Florida more resilient. They would cost a lot. But is those costs are not borne by Floridians through mixture of premiums, taxs and mandatory renovations to bring buildings up to code, they will either be passed to the fedaral government or they will note be paid, and the situation will detirorate. It would be fairer if those who enjoy the delights of living in Florida shoulder the bulk of the costs. Those who do not want to may opt to live somewhere else. Hard choices like this will proliferate as the world keeps warming.
